The Core Checklist

  • Licensed and insured -- ask for the license number and verify it at myfloridalicense.com
  • Dives the pool when conditions allow -- many companies do not get in the water
  • Uses pressure testing for plumbing lines
  • Uses acoustic listening equipment for underground leaks
  • Has trace gas capability for difficult underground breaks
  • Provides a written report with photos after every visit
  • Can explain the process step by step before you book
  • Is honest about what can and cannot be confirmed

Specialization matters. A company that does pool leak detection as its primary service is different from a general pool contractor who offers it on the side. The tools, process, and documentation standards are usually different too.
