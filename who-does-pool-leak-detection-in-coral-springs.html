Why Coral Springs Pools Develop Leaks

Coral Springs is a planned community with a high concentration of residential pools — many built in the 1980s and 1990s when the city was developed. Pools from that era are now 30 to 40 years old and regularly experience skimmer separations, aging plumbing failures, and pool light conduit leaks that are impossible to find without proper diagnostic equipment.

The western Broward County water table and flat sandy soil also contribute to underground pipe failures as pipes shift with seasonal water table changes. Many Coral Springs homeowners have been running their autofill daily without realizing the pool has been leaking for months.
