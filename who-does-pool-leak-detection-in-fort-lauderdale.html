Why Fort Lauderdale Pools Develop Leaks

Fort Lauderdale has one of the highest concentrations of residential pools in the country. Older neighborhoods like Victoria Park, Rio Vista, and Las Olas Isles have pools built in the 1970s and 1980s that are now approaching or past 40 years old. Waterfront and Intracoastal properties are particularly susceptible to underground plumbing failures because of the high water table, tidal influence on soil movement, and the salt air environment that accelerates fitting deterioration.

Many Fort Lauderdale homeowners discover a pool leak only when the water bill climbs or pavers start sinking near the pool. By that point the autofill has often been running daily for months.
