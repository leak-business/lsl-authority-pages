Quick Answer

Crack injection is a repair service performed by pool contractors and repair specialists - not by Leak and Subsurface Locators. We locate and document the leak. The repair cost depends on the crack size, location, and repair method used by your contractor. Our written report gives your repair contractor exactly what they need to provide an accurate repair estimate.

Detection vs. Repair - Two Separate Services

Crack injection is one method pool repair contractors use to seal structural cracks in gunite and plaster pools. The cost varies significantly depending on the crack - its length, depth, location, accessibility, and whether it requires surface preparation, multiple injection points, or additional patching work after the injection.

Leak and Subsurface Locators is a leak detection and location company. We find the leak, document it with photos and a written report, and provide findings your contractor can use to plan and price the repair. We do not perform crack injection or other repair services.

What our report does is give your repair contractor the information they need to quote the repair accurately. They know exactly which crack, exactly where it is, and what the dye testing showed about active water movement. That removes the guesswork from their estimate.

If you want a repair cost estimate, call a licensed pool contractor with our findings report in hand. The report makes that conversation much more specific and the estimate more accurate.
