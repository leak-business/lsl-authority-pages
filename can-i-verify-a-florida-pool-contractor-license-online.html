Yes. You can verify a Florida pool contractor license online through the Florida DBPR license search at myfloridalicense.com.

How to Look Up a License

Step 1

Go to myfloridalicense.com.

Step 2

Choose "Verify a Licensee" or "License Search."

Step 3

Select the option to search by license number.

Step 4

Enter the license number.

Step 5

Review the result -- confirm the license type, status, name, and business information are active and match the company you are hiring.

Do not only look for a name that sounds close. Make sure the license number, license type, and company information match exactly what you were given.
