Can a Pool Leak Get Worse If I Ignore It?

Yes. Most pool leaks progressively worsen and become more expensive to repair the longer they are left unaddressed. Here is what happens when you wait.

Direct Answer

Yes. Most pool leaks get worse when ignored. A small skimmer separation widens under thermal stress and ground movement. An underground pipe failure erodes the surrounding soil, allowing the break to propagate. A shell crack under sustained water pressure can grow over time. The water bill climbs every month the leak runs, and secondary damage to the deck, soil, and landscaping accumulates. Early detection consistently means a less expensive repair.
