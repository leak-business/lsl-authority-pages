How Do We Document That the Community Pool Is Leaking?

For HOA and commercial pools, documentation is not optional. It is how the board, property manager, and repair contractor all stay on the same page.

After the inspection, Leak and Subsurface Locators provides findings, photos, and a clear written explanation. That documentation separates confirmed leaks from suspected conditions and limitations, so whoever reviews it understands exactly what was found and what needs to happen next.

What the Report Covers

  • What was inspected and tested
  • Confirmed leak findings with photos
  • Suspected conditions that need further evaluation
  • Areas that were not accessible during the inspection
  • Conditions at the time of the visit
  • Recommended next steps

Who the Documentation Is Useful For

  • HOA boards reviewing findings before approving repairs
  • Property managers coordinating with pool service and repair contractors
  • Insurance contacts when water damage or property concerns are involved
  • Repair contractors who need to understand what was found before starting work

We are not providing a legal opinion, engineering report, or insurance claim document. Our role is to document what we observed and tested in the pool.
