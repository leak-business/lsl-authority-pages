Yes. Property managers are one of the groups we work with regularly, and we understand what they need.

A property manager dealing with a leaking community pool has a board to answer to, a pool service company to coordinate with, residents asking questions, and a water bill that is climbing. What they need from a leak detection company is not a quick opinion from someone who walked around the deck. They need documentation that holds up when it is reviewed by a board, another contractor, or a repair company.

What LSL Provides for Property Managers

  • Clear written findings with photos
  • Confirmed leaks separated from suspected conditions and limitations
  • Scheduling coordination around resident and guest use
  • Communication with pool service companies and maintenance contacts
  • Proof of licensing and insurance available upon request
  • Repair estimates when within scope, referrals when outside scope

For HOA, condo, hotel, and commercial properties, documentation is important. Leak and Subsurface Locators can provide findings, photos, and a clear explanation that a manager or board can review and act on.
