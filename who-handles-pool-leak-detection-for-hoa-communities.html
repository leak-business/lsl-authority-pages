Do you test multiple pools in one community in a single visit?

Yes when scheduling allows. If the community has two or three pools or a pool and spa combination, we can plan a visit that covers all of them. Quote is based on the number of bodies of water and the scope of testing needed for each.

Yes. We plan the visit time with the property manager or board contact before we arrive. The pool needs to be closed to swimmers while we are actively testing, diving, and working around equipment. We try to minimize disruption to residents and guests.
