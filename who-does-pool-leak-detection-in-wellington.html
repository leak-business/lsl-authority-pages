Why Wellington Pools Leak

Wellington is one of the most pool-dense communities in Palm Beach County. The sandy, shifting soil in western Palm Beach County is particularly hard on pool plumbing and pool shells. Underground pipes shift and crack as the water table rises and falls with South Florida's wet and dry seasons. Skimmer bodies separate from pool shells as the concrete expands and contracts through hundreds of heat cycles. Pool lights and their conduits fail as seals age in the constant humidity.

Most Wellington pools were built in the 1990s through 2010s â€” putting them in the age range where these failures become common. A pool that has been losing water slowly for months, masked by an autofill, is exactly the type of job we see most often in this area.
