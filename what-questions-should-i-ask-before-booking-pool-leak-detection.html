What Questions Should I Ask Before Booking Pool Leak Detection?

Ask these before you schedule anyone. The answers tell you a lot.

Licensing and Insurance

  • Are you licensed and insured?
  • What is your license number and what type of license is it?

Process and Equipment

  • Do you dive the pool?
  • Do you check the skimmer, lights, fittings, drains, equipment, and plumbing when needed?
  • Do you pressure test pool lines?
  • What do you do if the pool is too low, cloudy, or chemically unsafe to inspect properly?

Complex Pools

  • How do you handle pools with attached spas, water features, multiple pumps, or underground plumbing concerns?

Repairs and Reporting

  • Are repairs included in the detection price or are they separate?
  • Do you provide repair estimates when possible and within your scope?
  • What happens if the repair is outside your scope?
  • Do you provide a written report with photos?

For Real Estate, HOA, and Commercial Work

  • Can you provide documentation that agents, property managers, boards, or repair contractors can use?
  • Can you provide proof of licensing and insurance before the appointment?

A good company should be able to answer these questions clearly. If the answers are vague, rushed, or only about price, be careful.
