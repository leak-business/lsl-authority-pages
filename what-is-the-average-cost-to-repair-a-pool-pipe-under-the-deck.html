Quick Answer

Repairing a pool pipe under the deck is a job for a licensed pool or plumbing contractor - not for Leak and Subsurface Locators. We locate the break and document exactly where it is. Repair costs vary by location, depth, pipe size, and whether deck removal is required. A precise detection finding significantly reduces repair costs by eliminating unnecessary excavation.

Why Accurate Location Affects Repair Cost

Repairing an underground pool pipe under a deck involves excavating to reach the broken section, making the repair, and restoring the deck surface. The cost depends on several factors: how deep the pipe is buried, what the deck is made of (concrete, pavers, travertine, tile), how much deck needs to be removed to access the pipe, the pipe size and type, and what the repair requires - a simple coupling replacement or a longer section of pipe.

Accurate location is directly connected to repair cost. When a repair contractor knows exactly where the break is - within a foot or two of the actual location - they can remove the minimum amount of deck necessary to access the pipe. When a contractor is guessing, they may open multiple areas trying to find the break, each of which has to be restored afterward.

Leak and Subsurface Locators uses acoustic listening with professional ground microphones to pinpoint the break location along the deck. We run multiple test sequences to confirm the location before marking it. The marked location is what your contractor excavates.

For an accurate repair cost estimate, call a licensed pool contractor with our findings report. They will know the deck type, pipe depth, and repair scope required for your specific property and can give you an accurate number.

Our 30-day guarantee covers our findings. If the confirmed break location proves to be inaccurate and repairs are completed within 30 days, we address it. Good communication between the detection report and the repair contractor before excavation begins is the best way to avoid any discrepancy.
