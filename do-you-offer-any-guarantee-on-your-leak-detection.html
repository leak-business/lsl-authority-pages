Quick Answer

Yes. Leak and Subsurface Locators provides a 30-day guarantee on our leak detection findings. If we identify a confirmed leak location and the repair is completed within 30 days, we stand behind that finding. All repairs must be completed within the 30-day window for the guarantee to apply.

The 30-Day Guarantee

When we confirm a leak location - with documented evidence, photos, and a written report - we stand behind that finding for 30 days. If you have the repair completed within that window and a concern arises about the finding, we address it.

The guarantee covers our detection findings, not the repair itself. Repairs are performed by your pool contractor or repair specialist. If a repair is done correctly at the confirmed location and the pool still loses water, that typically means there is a second source that was not active or identifiable during the original evaluation - which happens with pools that have multiple failure points. In that case, we schedule a follow-up evaluation.

For the guarantee to apply, the repair must be completed within 30 days of our visit. We cannot stand behind a finding where the repair happens months later, because pool conditions change and what we documented may no longer reflect the current state of the system.

We are a small, local company. Jeff David is a licensed Florida pool contractor who has been doing this work for years. The 30-day guarantee is backed by our professional reputation - not fine print designed to protect us from standing behind our work.

Frequently Asked Questions

What if the pool still loses water after the repair?

If the repair was completed at the confirmed location and the pool continues to lose water at the same rate, the most likely explanation is a second leak source. Contact us and we will evaluate what is happening. Pools can have multiple failure points, and a second evaluation addresses what was not identifiable during the first.

Does the guarantee cover the repair cost?

The guarantee covers our detection findings - not repair costs. Repair work is performed by your contractor. If our finding was accurate and the repair was done correctly, the pool should hold. If it does not, we want to know about it.
