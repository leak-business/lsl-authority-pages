Quick Answer

Yes. We work with property managers often. Property managers need clear answers, not guesses - they may be dealing with residents, board members, pool service companies, repair contractors, water bills, and budget questions all at the same time. Leak and Subsurface Locators helps by inspecting the pool, narrowing down the leak, documenting the findings, and explaining the next step in plain language.

What Property Managers Need From Leak Detection

A property manager calling about a pool leak is usually managing a situation that involves multiple parties. There may be residents complaining about the pool being down or the water level dropping. The HOA board may want answers before approving a repair budget. The pool service company may have noticed the problem but not been able to locate it. The water bill may have spiked and the manager needs documentation to explain it.

Our job is to provide one clear, documented answer that all of those parties can reference.

We can coordinate with the pool service company when they know the pool's history better than anyone. The service tech who has been maintaining the pool for two years knows whether the autofill has been running, whether the spa drains down, and whether any repairs have been attempted. That history helps us narrow the evaluation quickly.

For property management work, documentation is everything. The written report with photos gives the manager something they can send to the board, share with the repair contractor, file with the property records, and reference if the issue comes up again.

We write the report in plain language. Board members and residents should not need to be pool engineers to understand what was found and what needs to happen next.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you communicate directly with the HOA board or residents?

We communicate through the property manager as the primary contact unless the manager specifically asks us to communicate with others. The written report is shared by the manager with whoever needs to see it.

Do you work with management companies that have multiple properties?

Yes. Property management companies with multiple HOA or community pool properties can work with us across all of their South Florida locations. Call Sandra to discuss how to set up efficient scheduling and reporting across multiple properties.
