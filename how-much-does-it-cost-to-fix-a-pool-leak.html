Repair cost depends entirely on what is leaking and where. A skimmer seal and an underground pipe repair are very different jobs. Here is what drives the price.

Typical Repair Cost Ranges by Type

These are general ranges to give you a realistic expectation. Actual pricing depends on the specific conditions at your pool â€” pipe depth, deck surface, access, and the extent of the failure. We quote your repair after confirming the source during the detection visit.

Skimmer sealing

Lower cost range

A minor skimmer separation sealed with appropriate waterproofing material. Straightforward when caught early.

Return fitting repair

Lower cost range

Replacement of a leaking eyeball port, vacuum port, or return fitting. Usually straightforward access from inside the pool.

Light conduit sealing

Lower to mid range

Sealing the conduit path through which water exits the light niche. Scope varies by conduit condition.

Crack injection

Mid range

Material and labor for injecting a confirmed leaking structural crack. Ranges with crack length, location, and depth.

Skimmer replacement

Mid to higher range

Full skimmer body removal and replacement when sealing is not sufficient. Includes partial drain and deck work around the skimmer.

Underground pipe repair

Higher range

Excavation, pipe repair, and backfill. Cost scales significantly with pipe depth, deck surface material, and extent of the pipe failure.

What Makes Underground Pipe Repair More Expensive

Underground pipe repairs are the highest-cost pool leak repair category because the pipe failure is buried under concrete, travertine, soil, or a combination of all three. The excavation itself â€” cutting through surface material, digging to the pipe, opening the repair, backfilling, and surface restoration â€” is often more expensive than the actual pipe repair.

This is why targeted locating before any cutting is so important. A pipe repair on a pool with plain concrete deck and a pipe 18 inches down costs substantially less than the same repair on a travertine deck with a pipe 36 inches down. The leak detection and locating visit gives you an accurate repair estimate because we know the exact location, depth, and deck type before we quote.

Why Early Detection Saves Repair Money

A skimmer separation found in its first season is a sealing job. Found after two or three seasons of widening, it is a skimmer replacement. An underground pipe failure found before significant soil erosion is a single targeted excavation. Found after a year of running and washing out the deck substrate, the repair bill adds void filling and deck restoration on top of the pipe repair.
