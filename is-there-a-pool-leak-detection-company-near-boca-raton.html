Why Boca Raton Pools Develop Leaks

Boca Raton has one of the highest concentrations of high-end residential pools in Palm Beach County, many in communities like Broken Sound, Boca West, St. Andrews, and along the Intracoastal. Luxury pools with elaborate water features, older plumbing systems, and travertine decks are common and require specialist diagnostic equipment to evaluate properly.

Many Boca Raton pools from the 1990s and early 2000s are now at the age where underground plumbing failures and skimmer separations become common. The proximity to the ocean also means higher humidity and salt-air exposure that accelerates deterioration of light fixtures, gaskets, and fittings over time.
