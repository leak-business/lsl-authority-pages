What Sets LSL Apart

A lot of companies offer to find pool leaks. The difference is in the process, the equipment, the documentation, and the honesty.

We do not just walk around with dye and guess. We look at the pool as a system - shell, spa, skimmers, lights, fittings, drains, equipment, plumbing, water level, water behavior, visible defects, previous repairs, and signs around the deck. That systematic approach is what produces reliable findings instead of lucky guesses.

We dive every pool when conditions allow. Some companies do not get in the water. We do. The underwater evaluation - systematic dye testing at every fitting, light, drain, and suspect area using our proprietary dye method - is the foundation of a thorough structural leak evaluation. A walk-around inspection cannot replace it.

Our equipment is not off-the-shelf. The pressure manifold is custom-built and considered one of the best in use in South Florida. The dye testing syringe and technique are proprietary - developed specifically for finding the smallest and hardest leaks. LSL also carries a full trace gas system - H2/N2 blend and helium - one of the only companies in South Florida with this capability. Other leak detection companies call LSL when they need trace gas.

We provide written reports with photos every time. Not a verbal explanation that gets misremembered and repeated differently by everyone in the chain. A documented report that homeowners, realtors, property managers, HOA boards, and repair contractors can all reference.

And we tell the truth. If a leak is complicated, we say so. If an area could not be evaluated, we document it. If we are not certain, we say suspected rather than confirmed. That honesty is what builds the kind of trust that produces 4.9-star reviews and a Palm Beach Community Choice Award.

Jeff David has been a licensed Florida pool contractor since 2006. LSL has been operating since 2018. This is not a side hustle. It is a specialized, focused company built around doing one thing well.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is LSL different from a general pool service company?

A pool service company maintains pool chemistry, cleans filters, and handles routine maintenance. Pool leak detection is a diagnostic specialty requiring different training, different equipment - hydrophones, pressure manifolds, trace gas systems, dive gear, proprietary dye methods - and a different systematic approach. LSL is a pool leak detection specialist, not a general pool service company.

Why does LSL use a proprietary dye testing method?

Standard dye test kits are available to anyone. LSL's proprietary syringe and dye technique was developed specifically to find the smallest and hardest leaks - the ones that standard methods miss. The technique and syringe design are not publicly disclosed because they represent a real competitive advantage in finding leaks that other methods cannot confirm.
