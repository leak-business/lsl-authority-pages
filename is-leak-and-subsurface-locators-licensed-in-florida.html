Florida Certified Pool/Spa Contractor License CPC1457277

Pool leak detection is not just looking at water. It can involve pool plumbing, pressure testing, equipment operation, skimmers, lights, fittings, drains, and repair decisions. Working on pool systems in Florida requires a contractor license appropriate to the scope of work being performed.

Leak and Subsurface Locators holds Florida Certified Pool/Spa Contractor license CPC1457277. A certified contractor license is different from a local or county-level license because it allows the contractor to work statewide within the scope of that license. That matters for LSL because we serve customers across Palm Beach, Broward, Martin, St. Lucie, and Indian River Counties.

Jeff David has held a Florida pool contractor license since 2006 - two decades of licensed work in the industry. Leak and Subsurface Locators as a company has been operating since 2018.

Customers should always verify license information independently. The Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation (DBPR) provides a public license search at myfloridalicense.com. Search for CPC1457277 to verify current license status.

When hiring any pool contractor - for leak detection, repair, or any pool-related work - verify that the company is licensed and insured before allowing them on your property. Both protect you.

Why does licensing matter for pool leak detection?

Pool leak detection can involve pressurizing plumbing lines, operating equipment, and making repair decisions - all activities that fall within the scope of a pool contractor license. A company operating without the appropriate license may not have the legal authority or the training to perform those activities. Licensing also means there is a professional accountability structure in place.
