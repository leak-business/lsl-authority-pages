How the Recheck Process Works

If a repair is needed, that repair must be completed before a no-charge recheck is performed under the limited 30-day leak detection warranty. One known active leak can hide another leak. Once the known leak is addressed, the pool can be evaluated more fairly if water loss continues.

Repairs performed by LSL include a 1-year warranty on our repair and repair connections. That warranty applies to the repair work we perform. It does not mean the entire pool is guaranteed not to leak from another area later.
