No trip fee anywhere inside our normal five-county service area.

Leak and Subsurface Locators does not charge a separate trip fee inside our normal service area.

Our normal service area includes cities and communities in Palm Beach County, Broward County, Martin County, St. Lucie County, and Indian River County. If your property is inside the area we normally serve, there is not an extra travel charge just because you are outside Palm Beach County.

What Can Still Change the Price

The price can still change because of the pool itself. A large pool, spa, water feature, zero edge, multiple pumps, complicated plumbing, HOA pool, commercial pool, or difficult access may need a separate estimate.

Getting a Fast Quote

For most residential pools, we can usually quote the job by phone. Photos of the pool and equipment help us give a more accurate answer before we schedule.
