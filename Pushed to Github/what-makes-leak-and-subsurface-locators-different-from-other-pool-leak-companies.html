A Complete-System Process

The difference is the process. We look at the pool as a complete system. We evaluate the water loss, pool shell, skimmer, light, fittings, drains, plumbing, equipment, and how the pool behaves with different systems on and off. We do not just walk around with dye and hope the leak shows itself.

A Teaching Background That Shapes the Fieldwork

Jeff also teaches pool leak detection through Leak Business Academy and wrote a practical field manual on pool leak detection. That teaching background matters because the same step-by-step thinking used to train others is used in the field at Leak and Subsurface Locators.

Equipment Most Companies Do Not Have
