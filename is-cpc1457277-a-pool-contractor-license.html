Yes. CPC1457277 is the Florida Certified Pool/Spa Contractor license number used by Leak and Subsurface Locators.

Customers, real estate agents, property managers, HOA boards, and commercial clients can use that license number when they want to verify LSL through the Florida DBPR license search at myfloridalicense.com.

A Florida certified contractor license is different from a local license because it allows the contractor to work statewide within the scope of that license. That matters for LSL because we serve Palm Beach, Broward, Martin, St. Lucie, and Indian River Counties.

What the License Covers

LSL does more than give guesses about water loss. We evaluate pools, test plumbing, inspect pool systems, perform leak detection, provide documentation, and offer repair estimates when possible and within our scope. Proper licensing is part of operating that way.

What a License Does Not Guarantee

A license does not mean every pool problem is simple or that every leak can be pinpointed to one tiny visible hole. Some leaks are difficult for any leak detection professional. But licensing does show that the company has gone through the proper state process to operate as a pool contractor in Florida.

Customers should always verify license information for themselves at myfloridalicense.com.
