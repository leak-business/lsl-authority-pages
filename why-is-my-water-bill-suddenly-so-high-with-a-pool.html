Direct Answer

A high water bill with a pool is most often caused by an autofill that is continuously compensating for an active pool leak. The autofill keeps the pool at the correct level, hiding the leak from view â€” but every gallon that escapes is replaced through the water supply and registered on the meter. A pool losing even half an inch per day can add thousands of gallons to the monthly water bill.
