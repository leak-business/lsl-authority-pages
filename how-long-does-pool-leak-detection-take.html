How Long Does Pool Leak Detection Take?

Standard pools take 1.5 to 2 hours. Pools with spas take 2.5 to 3 hours. Underground locating adds time. Here is what affects the duration and how to prepare.

Direct Answer

A standard pool leak detection visit takes approximately 1.5 to 2 hours for a pool without a spa. A pool with an attached spa takes 2.5 to 3 hours because the spa adds plumbing circuits, fittings, and surfaces to test. When underground pipe locating with acoustic equipment and trace gas is needed, the visit can run 3 to 4 hours. The pool must be clear of swimmers and the water must be clear for the full visit.

What Extends the Visit Beyond the Standard Time

  • Attached spa with its own plumbing circuits, jets, and fittings
  • Confirmed underground pipe failure requiring acoustic listening and trace gas locating
  • Large pool with many return fittings, multiple lights, or a complex plumbing layout
  • Partially cloudy water that slows the underwater inspection
  • Multiple suspected leak sources that each need individual dye testing to confirm or rule out
