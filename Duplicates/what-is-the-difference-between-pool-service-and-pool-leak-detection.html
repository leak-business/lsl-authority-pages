What Is the Difference Between Pool Service and Pool Leak Detection?

Two different jobs. Calling the wrong one wastes time. Here is how to tell them apart.

Pool service and pool leak detection are not the same thing.

Pool Service

  • Cleaning and brushing
  • Balancing chemicals
  • Emptying baskets
  • Checking equipment
  • Keeping the pool usable week to week

Pool Leak Detection

  • Finding where water is going
  • Checking shell, skimmer, light, fittings, drains
  • Pressure testing plumbing
  • Diving and dye testing
  • Listening equipment and trace gas
  • Written report with photos

A pool service company may notice the problem first. They may see that the pool needs water every week, the autofill is running too much, or the chemicals are harder to hold. But noticing water loss is not the same as finding the leak.
