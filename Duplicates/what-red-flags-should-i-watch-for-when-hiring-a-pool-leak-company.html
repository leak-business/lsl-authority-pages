What Red Flags Should I Watch for When Hiring a Pool Leak Company?

Specific warning signs to watch for before you book anyone.

Red Flags to Watch For

  • Acts like every leak is simple. Pool leaks are not always simple. Some only happen when the pump is on. Some only show when the pump is off. Some are in the plumbing. Some are in the shell. Some pools have more than one leak.
  • Cheap price but cannot explain the process. Ask if they dive the pool, pressure test plumbing, check skimmers, lights, fittings, drains, equipment, and underground lines. Ask if they provide photos and a report.
  • Wants to cut concrete, lift pavers, or replace parts before confirming the leak. Nobody should be digging or replacing major components before the leak has been properly narrowed down.
  • Cannot explain their process step by step. If they cannot walk you through what they will do and in what order, that is a problem.
  • No photos, no written report. A company that does not document their work leaves the customer with nothing to review or share.

Also be careful with big promises. Not every leak can be pinpointed to one tiny visible hole. Pebble finish pools, mosaic tile pools, older pools, previous repairs, and shell leaks can be difficult for any leak detection professional. A good company is honest about those limits.

What to Look For Instead

A good leak detection company should be licensed, insured, clear about pricing, willing to explain the findings, and honest about what can and cannot be confirmed.
