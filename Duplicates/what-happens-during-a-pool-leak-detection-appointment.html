What Happens During a Pool Leak Detection Appointment?

Here is the real sequence from arrival to final explanation.

Step 1

Look and Listen First

When Leak and Subsurface Locators arrives, the first thing we do is look and listen. We want to understand what the customer has seen, how much water the pool is losing, whether the pump has been running, whether there is an autofill, and whether there are any obvious wet areas or recent repairs.

Step 2

Evaluate the Pool as a System

Then we look at the pool and equipment as a system. We check the pool area, water level, skimmer, lights, fittings, drains, spa, water features if present, and the equipment pad. We look for signs that help tell us where the water may be going.

Step 3

Test Systematically

From there, we test systematically. Depending on the pool, that may include dye testing, underwater inspection, checking the skimmer and light niche, evaluating fittings, checking the equipment, pressure testing plumbing lines, listening equipment, and trace gas when the situation calls for it. We may test with the pump on, pump off, or with certain parts of the system isolated.

Step 4

Explain What Was Found

At the end, we explain what we found, what we could confirm, what may need repair, and what the next step should be. If we find a simple leak point that can be temporarily patched with a small epoxy patch, we may do that when appropriate. If the repair is bigger or outside our scope, we explain that and may recommend trusted professionals.
