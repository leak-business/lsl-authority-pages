$425

Starting price for a standard residential leak detection visit

A standard residential pool leak detection visit with Leak and Subsurface Locators starts at $425.

That starting price is for a normal residential pool around 15 feet by 30 feet, with one pump, no waterfalls, no zero edge, no major water features, and normal access to the pool and equipment. It also includes many standard pool and spa combinations, including pools with a booster pump for the spa jets.

What Can Raise the Price

  • Larger pools
  • Water features or waterfalls
  • Zero edge pools
  • Multiple pumps
  • Complex plumbing
  • Unusual equipment setups
  • HOA or commercial pools
  • Difficult access to the pool or equipment

Pools that fall into any of these categories may require a separate estimate rather than the standard starting price.

Getting an Accurate Quote

Most of the time, we can give a good estimate over the phone. Current photos of the pool, spa, water features, and equipment pad help a lot. We can sometimes pull aerial images of the pool, but we prefer pictures from the customer because they show what is actually there right now.

Prices do change over time. Pool leak detection is a skilled service that takes time, equipment, and experience. The best way to get the right price is to call Leak and Subsurface Locators and explain what kind of pool you have.
