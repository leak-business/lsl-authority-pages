Should I Call a Pool Leak Specialist or My Regular Pool Company?

Each has a different job. Here is how to know which call to make.

Call Your Pool Company When

  • Routine cleaning and chemicals
  • Filter service
  • Basic equipment checks
  • Normal weekly maintenance

Call a Specialist When

  • Pool keeps losing water
  • Autofill runs constantly
  • Spa drains down overnight
  • Water bill jumped unexpectedly
  • Nobody can explain where the water is going

Leak detection is a different job. It may require diving, dye testing, pressure testing, listening equipment, trace gas, equipment evaluation, and a systematic process to narrow down the source. That is not what routine pool service is designed to do.

How the Two Work Together

A good pool service company can be a great partner in the process. They often know the pool history better than anyone -- when the water loss started, how often water is being added, and whether something changed with the equipment.

At LSL, we work with pool service companies often. We are not there to replace them. We are there to solve the leak question.
