Do You Need Access to My House or Just the Backyard?

Almost always backyard only. Here is the one exception.

(954) 290-5177 Broward (561) 325-2678 Palm Beach

Most of the time, we only need access to the backyard, pool, spa, equipment pad, and any gates or side yards needed to reach the work area.

There are times when inside access may be helpful or necessary. The most common reason is the electrical panel. If equipment needs to be safely shut down and the breaker is inside the garage or house, we may need access to that area. Sometimes automation controls, pool equipment controls, or other related systems are also inside or in a garage.

Helpful Things to Know Before the Appointment

  • Where the pool equipment is located
  • Where the electrical panel is located
  • Whether the gate is unlocked
  • Whether there are pets in the yard

Real Estate, Rentals, HOAs, and Vacant Homes

For real estate inspections, rentals, HOAs, and vacant homes, access is very important. If we cannot access the equipment, pool area, or controls, the evaluation may be limited.
