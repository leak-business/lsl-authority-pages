Should I Fill the Pool Before the Leak Detection Appointment?

In most cases yes - but do not change things without calling first.

In most cases, yes. We want the pool at a normal operating level before the appointment.

If the water is too low, we may not be able to test the skimmer, lights, fittings, tile line, spa spillover, or other areas properly. A pool that is too low can limit the inspection and make the leak harder to confirm.

That said, do not overfill it or try to fix the situation before we get there. If the pool keeps dropping to the same level, that information is useful. If the water is below the skimmer, light, or fittings, take photos before filling it so we can see where it stopped.

The Best Rule

When you book, we will usually ask about the current water level and how fast it is dropping. Based on that, we can tell you whether to fill it, leave it alone, or take pictures first.

Call before making big changes. The water level tells a story, and we do not want to lose useful clues.
