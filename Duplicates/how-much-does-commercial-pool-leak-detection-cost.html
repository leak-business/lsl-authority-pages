How Much Does Commercial Pool Leak Detection Cost?

Commercial pricing is custom. Here is what drives it and how to get a real number.

Commercial pool leak detection is usually estimated separately.

The standard $425 residential starting price is for a normal residential pool around 15 feet by 30 feet, one pump, no major water features, and normal access. Commercial pools are usually different. They are larger, busier, have more equipment, more plumbing, more liability concerns, and often require coordination with managers, pool companies, boards, residents, or guests.

What Drives Commercial Pricing

  • Pool size
  • Access
  • Number of bodies of water
  • Equipment layout
  • Plumbing complexity
  • Water features
  • Scheduling needs
  • Whether evaluation needs to happen during lower-use hours

We do not want to give a one-size-fits-all commercial price because it would not be fair to the customer or to us. Some commercial pools are simple. Others are a full investigation.

Getting a Real Estimate

The best way to price commercial leak detection is to send pictures of the pool, equipment, water features, and any visible problem areas. Then we can talk through the situation and give a realistic estimate.
