Could a Pool Leak Make My Pavers or Deck Settle?

Yes. Here is the connection and what to do first.

(954) 290-5177 Broward (561) 325-2678 Palm Beach

Yes, a pool leak can contribute to pavers or deck settlement, but it is not the only possible cause.

Water going where it does not belong can wash out soil, soften areas under the deck, move sand under pavers, or make existing settlement more noticeable. If the leak continues for a long time, the area around the pool can start showing signs like sinking pavers, hollow spots, cracks, loose coping, wet soil, or uneven deck sections.

Deck movement can come from many things, including drainage, soil conditions, poor compaction, tree roots, construction issues, age, or water loss. But if the pool is also losing water, the leak needs to be evaluated.

The Right First Step

The right first step is to find out whether the pool or pool plumbing is contributing to the problem. Leak detection can help answer that question.

At Leak and Subsurface Locators, we look at the pool, plumbing, equipment, visible wet areas, deck clues, and water loss pattern. If the issue points beyond leak detection, we explain that and may recommend trusted professionals for the repair or further evaluation.
