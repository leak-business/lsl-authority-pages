What Happens After You Find the Pool Leak?

A report, photos, a clear explanation, and the right next step. Here is what to expect.

After Leak and Subsurface Locators finds the leak, we explain what we found and what should happen next.

Step 1

If the customer is there, we explain the finding before we leave. We show where the leak was found, how we confirmed it, and whether it is a simple patch, a repair within our scope, or something that needs a different professional.

Step 2

We provide a report with photos. That report helps the homeowner, realtor, property manager, HOA board, or repair contractor understand what was found. Some leaks photograph clearly. Others are confirmed by dye movement, pressure testing, water behavior, listening equipment, or trace gas. Either way, we document the finding as clearly as we can.

Step 3

We explain the repair path. LSL offers repairs and provides estimates when possible and within our scope. If the repair is outside our scope, we recommend trusted professionals. If a small epoxy patch is appropriate, we may apply it on the spot to stop or slow the leak and confirm the leak point.

What the Customer Does With the Report

  • Schedule a repair with LSL when within our scope
  • Call one of the trusted professionals we recommend
  • Share findings with a realtor during a transaction
  • Send it to a property manager or HOA board for review
  • Use it to plan the next step before any repair work begins

Broken plumbing, large leaks, structural leaks, shell leaks, and leaks that may require waterproofing need a different repair path. The report explains which category the finding falls into so the right decision can be made.
