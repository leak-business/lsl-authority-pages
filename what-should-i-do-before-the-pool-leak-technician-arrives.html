What Should I Do Before the Pool Leak Technician Arrives?

A practical checklist so the visit goes smoothly from the start.

(954) 290-5177 Broward (561) 325-2678 Palm Beach

Before Leak and Subsurface Locators arrives, the main thing is to make sure we can access everything we need.

Access

  • Gate unlocked and pool area accessible
  • Equipment pad clear and reachable
  • Gate codes, guard gate info, or parking instructions sent ahead of time
  • Pets secured before we arrive

Pool and Equipment

  • Pool filled to proper operating level (unless we told you otherwise)
  • If water dropped and stopped at a certain level, take photos before filling
  • Working hose available on site
  • Working electrical outlet accessible
  • Pump left running (do not shut it off unless there is a safety issue)

Information to Have Ready

  • How much water is the pool losing, and how often are you adding water?
  • Does it lose more with the pump on or off?
  • Does the spa drain down overnight?
  • Is there an autofill?
  • Has anyone repaired the pool recently?

The more we know before we start, the better.
