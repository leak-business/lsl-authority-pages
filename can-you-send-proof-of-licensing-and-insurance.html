Licensing and Insurance Documentation

Professional properties - HOA communities, condo associations, hotels, commercial facilities, and property management accounts - routinely require vendor licensing and insurance documentation before allowing any contractor on site. That is a reasonable practice and we support it.

Leak and Subsurface Locators holds Florida Certified Pool/Spa Contractor license CPC1457277. License information can be verified independently through the Florida DBPR at myfloridalicense.com.

For insurance documentation, we can provide a certificate of insurance when requested. The request typically needs to come through Sandra when scheduling so we know who to address the certificate to and where to send it.

This should be standard practice - not an unusual request. If a company is coming to inspect or work on your pool, especially at a community or commercial property, you should be able to verify who they are, confirm their license is current, and confirm they carry appropriate insurance. Any professional contractor should be prepared to provide that documentation without hesitation.
