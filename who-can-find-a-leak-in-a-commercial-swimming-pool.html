A licensed pool leak detection specialist with experience in commercial properties is the right call for a commercial swimming pool.

Commercial pools require the same core detection process as residential pools -- underwater inspection, dye testing, pressure testing, acoustic listening equipment, and trace gas when needed. But the environment is different. Commercial pools are larger, busier, and involve more stakeholders.

Community and commercial pools are one of LSL's specialties. We coordinate with property managers, pool service companies, board contacts, and maintenance staff. We schedule around bather use when possible and provide written findings with photos that boards and managers can review and act on.

Leak and Subsurface Locators holds Florida Certified Pool/Spa Contractor license CPC1457277, a statewide certified license that allows work across all five service counties. For commercial and HOA work, proof of licensing and insurance is available upon request.
